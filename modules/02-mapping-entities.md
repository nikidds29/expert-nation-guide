# Mapping Entities: from Heurist to Omeka

Heurist described a person's life through many differently-named
relationships, such as *was ordained*, *married*, or *resided in*.
In Omeka, each of those becomes its own field on the Person record,
linking out to a smaller record instead of naming the relationship
directly.

| Heurist | Omeka |
|---|---|
| Record type | Resource template |
| Record | Item |
